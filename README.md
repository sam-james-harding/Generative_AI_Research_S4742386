# Using Generative AI Tools - Boon or Bane
This repository contains all the files used for the study "Using Generative AI Tools - Boon or Bane".

1. Explain why it is important to store and organise your files efficiently.
It is important to store and organise your files efficiently so that you can easily locate your work and research data, and so that you do not lose your work. An effective organisation method (file naming conventions and folder structures) aids in quickly locating relevant work, and keeping track of what kind of data is stored and when it was collected. Additionally, storage methods that allow you to back up your work and provide version control allow you to avoid losing your work, to identify when work/data was added, and aid in locating when errors/bugs were introduced.

2. Specify which data types may require special access controls. 
In this scenario, the types of data most likely to require special access controls are the qualitative and quantitative data, and potentially the reports/drafts. Where the data contains identifying or confidential information, its security is important to maintain the privacy of any study participants (this could apply to both qualitative data, such as interviews, but also quantitative data if it is connected to an identifier or could reasonably identify a participant). If the reports/drafts refer to this information in a way that could identify participants (or the research is under some form of confidentiality requirements) then special access controls may be required as well.

3. Why is it important to have a good naming convention?
It is important to have a good naming convention as it allows you to organise your files in a systematic manner, and makes locating and organising files simpler. For example, if the naming convention was <DOCUMENT_TYPE>_<DOCUMENT_TOPIC>_<AUTHOR_NAME>_<DATE> then simply by looking at the filename, you can identify what kind of document it is, when it was created and by who. If this convention is used consistently, it also makes searching for files by a particular trait (e.g. type of document) much easier.
